Joule — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joule handles information when you use the Joule Discord bot.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled on a server, Joule may process limited Discord-related information necessary to provide its features.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Usernames or display names
  • Roles and permissions required for bot functionality
  • AFK status and AFK messages
  • Moderation-related information when moderation features are used
  • Bot configuration and server settings

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used only as necessary to operate Joule and provide requested features, including:

  • Providing welcome and goodbye messages
  • Providing AFK functionality
  • Providing security and moderation features
  • Maintaining server-specific bot settings
  • Preventing abuse and misuse
  • Improving reliability and functionality

3. Messages and Content

Depending on the enabled features, Joule may process message information necessary to perform moderation, security, AFK, or other requested commands. Joule does not intend to use message content for advertising or sell user information.

4. Data Storage

Joule aims to store only information reasonably necessary for its features. The type and duration of storage may depend on the specific feature and server configuration.

5. Data Sharing

Joule does not sell personal information. Information may be processed through third-party infrastructure or services required to operate the bot.

6. Discord

Joule operates on Discord and may receive information through Discord's platform. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Data Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your use of Joule or would like to request deletion of stored information, contact the Joule community staff through the official Joule Discord server.

8. Children's Privacy

Joule does not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide its Discord features. Users should follow Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Joule community staff through the official Joule Discord server.
